MINISTRY: Christ The Rod - Ministry & Administration

CHURCH ADMINISTRATION RULES:
1. Order of Service: Opening Prayer, Praise & Worship, Announcements, Offering, Firstfruit/Thanksgiving Seed and Alter Seed, Testimony, Normal Offering and Tithe, Choir Ministration, Sermon, Closing Prayer/Benediction.
2. Membership: Record name, address, phone, baptism status, department.
3. Finance: Two people count offering, record, bank. Transparency is godliness.
4. Departments: Ushers, Choir, Youth, Media, Sunday School, Protocol.
5. Leadership: Pastor, Elders, Deacons, Decones, Chairlady, Chairman, Ministers, Ministries, Disciples, Secretary. Respect hierarchy.

ADMINISTRATION PRINCIPLE: Everything should be done decently and in order. 1 Corinthians 14:40
