AUTHOR: Boima Musa Tamba, Mining & Mineral Engineer, KNUST, Kumasi. From New London 2, Koidu, Kono, Sierra Leone.

CORE PRINCIPLE: Kono lives on open-pits, but our pits are killing our brothers because we dig straight down with no design.

A SAFE PIT MUST HAVE 4 THINGS:
1. BENCHES: Steps every 3 meters high, minimum 2 meters wide. Never vertical wall. Benches stop collapse.
2. SLOPE: 45 degrees maximum for Kono soil (laterite and gravel). Steep walls = collapse. Test: If soil is loose sand, use 35 degrees.
3. HAUL ROAD: 10 meters wide minimum. For trucks and for escape when pit collapses or floods. Spiral road around pit.
4. DRAINAGE: 1m deep channel around pit and sump at bottom. Pump water out. Stop mining when heavy rain. Rainwater is #1 cause of collapse in Tankoro.

MY SOLUTION / WORKFLOW:
Step 1: Survey first with tape and compass.
Step 2: Cut benches from top to bottom.
Step 3: Keep slope at 45 degrees.
Step 4: Dig drainage channel before mining gravel.
Step 5: Stop mining in heavy rain (July-August).
Step 6: Safety: Helmet, rope, boots, buddy system. No one mines alone.
Step 7: Backfill after mining. Restore land.

DANGER SIGNS (AI must warn):
- Cracks on top edge of pit
- Water seeping from wall
- Vertical wall >3m with no bench
- No escape road
- People mining under overhang

We don't stop mining, we mine with engineers.
